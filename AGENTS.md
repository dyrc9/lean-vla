# Project status reporting

For project-progress or project-status requests, read
`docs/current_status_and_roadmap.md` first and organize the answer only around:

1. the successfully reproduced SABER attack and its observed attack-success rate;
2. the final ProofAlign system and its paired four-arm result;
3. the project closeout state, with the paper source and evidence frozen;
4. reproducibility-only maintenance, with no further experiments planned unless the user explicitly reopens the project.

Treat ProofAlign as one final prototype. Do not enumerate internal versions, optimization stages, archived failed experiments,
or intermediate results unless the user explicitly asks for history, failure analysis, or a specific frozen artifact.

Preserve historical protocols, results, checksums, and non-pass classifications for reproducibility. Documentation cleanup
does not authorize deleting or reinterpreting those artifacts. Historical classifications belong in audit material rather than
the default paper narrative; the default narrative reports the observed SABER reproduction result and its measured ASR.
